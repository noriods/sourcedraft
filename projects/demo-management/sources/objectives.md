# Course objectives: MGT 101 Principles of Management, week 2

## Learning objectives
By the end of this session students can:
1. Describe the basic characteristics of managerial decision-making.
2. Contrast the reflective and reactive systems of decision-making.
3. Distinguish programmed from nonprogrammed decisions.
4. Identify barriers to effective decision-making, including confirmation bias.
5. Apply techniques that improve individual and group decisions.

## Instructor preferences
Slides stay short for students. Teaching guidance goes in instructor notes. Case discussion should end on a decision the students must make.
